Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy explains how MealFold handles personal data. The controller is Umberto Alinei, based in Italy. Contact umberto.alinei@icloud.com for privacy requests and support.

Using MealFold as a guest

Your guest profile, selected photos, personal recipes, favorites, meal plan and grocery list are stored on your iPhone. The online catalog receives the searches and image requests needed to return recipes. Network providers receive technical connection information such as IP addresses. Apple may include local app data in device backups according to your iCloud and device settings.

Sign in with Apple and synchronization

Signing in is optional. When you sign in, Apple and our authentication provider process the identifiers and credentials needed to authenticate you. The service may receive the name and email address Apple provides, including a private relay address. Apple does not provide your Apple Account profile photo to MealFold.

With an account, MealFold synchronizes your profile and photos, personal recipes, favorites, meal plan and grocery list. Guest data can be copied automatically into the first associated account on this iPhone after sign-in. The original guest data remains local. Copying it to a different account requires confirmation. Do not import guest content into an account that should not receive it.

Why we process data

  • To provide the catalog, authentication and synchronization you request. The legal basis is performance of the service agreement under GDPR Article 6(1)(b).
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse and diagnose service failures. The basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure service under Article 6(1)(f), balanced against your rights.
  • To respond to support and rights requests and meet applicable legal obligations, using the basis appropriate to that request, including Article 6(1)(c) where required.

MealFold does not sell personal data or include advertising SDKs or cross-company advertising tracking. It does not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Enter only information needed for your recipes. The app is not a medical service and does not require health records.

Providers and international transfers

Supabase provides database, authentication and file storage infrastructure. Apple provides Sign in with Apple and the operating-system services you choose. GitHub Pages hosts these support pages. Their authorized service providers may process technical data to deliver and secure these services. Email support is handled through the support mailbox provider.

Providers may process data in countries outside the European Economic Area. Where applicable, transfers rely on an adequacy decision or appropriate contractual safeguards, including standard contractual clauses. You may request information about the safeguards applicable to your data. See Supabase’s data processing terms, Apple’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Sharing and access

Your synchronized workspace is private to your account under the service’s access policies. Authorized administrators and infrastructure providers may access data when necessary to operate, secure or support the service. This is not end-to-end encrypted storage. Do not upload secrets or sensitive documents.

Sharing a recipe exports the content you choose to share to the destination selected in the iOS share sheet. Recipients can keep an independent copy that is not removed when you later edit or delete your own recipe. Public catalog recipes are editorial content. MealFold does not offer a public feed of users’ private recipes.

Retention and account deletion

Local guest data remains until you remove it through the app or your device’s storage controls, subject to device backups. Account data is retained while your account is active to provide the service. You can export data and initiate account deletion in Profile → Account. Account deletion revokes the Apple authorization and removes the account and synchronized private records and photos. Failed steps are retried safely and the app shows whether deletion is still pending.

A completion receipt can be retained for 30 days to recover from a lost deletion response. Completed receipts do not retain the account identifier or Apple credentials. Technical logs are retained only as needed for security and troubleshooting under the service configuration. Backup copies expire according to the configured backup cycle and are not used for normal account access. If a backup is restored, completed deletion requests must be reconciled before normal service resumes.

Support correspondence is retained for the time needed to resolve the request and address related follow-up or legal obligations. You may ask for further information about retention applicable to your request.

Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and object to processing based on legitimate interests. If a processing operation relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact the controller using the email above. You may complain to your supervisory authority, including Italy’s Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Photos, permissions and children

The system photo picker lets you select photos to use in MealFold. Granting an iOS permission is separate from any legal basis for processing. The app is not directed at children and is not marketed in the App Store Kids category. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data inappropriately.

This website and policy changes

These MealFold pages use no analytics, advertising cookies, contact forms or third-party fonts. Hosting may process technical access logs. Links to other websites and email open services with their own policies. Material changes to this policy will be reflected in the date above and communicated where required.
