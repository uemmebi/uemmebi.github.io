NutriGo support

Recipes, meal plans and groceries on your iPhone. NutriGo is free and has no in-app purchases.

For help, accessibility issues or privacy requests, email umberto.alinei@icloud.com. Include your iOS and app versions, the steps you followed and any error message. Never send passwords, Apple verification codes, API keys or your full data export.

Move to a new iPhone

On the old iPhone, sign in with Apple and wait for synchronization to finish. Sign in with the same Apple Account on the new iPhone. Guest-only data remains on its original device until imported into an account.

Use recipes offline

Saved recipes remain available without a connection. Changes made offline are synchronized when the app is online again. Online catalog search and account actions require a connection.

Share and import a recipe

Open your personal recipe and use Share. Send the .nutrigo file. Open a received file in NutriGo, or choose Import recipe, and confirm the import. The recipient gets an independent copy. Recipe files contain ingredients and instructions; a private photo is not included.

Resolve a synchronization conflict

If the same item changes on two devices, open Account and review the conflicting versions. Choose the version to keep. Do not repeatedly delete and reinstall the app to resolve a conflict.

Export or delete your account

Open Profile → Account. Export lets you save your data. Delete account requests Apple confirmation and removes the synchronized account and private data. If interrupted, reopen the app online to let deletion resume. Apple may send a revocation email. Guest originals and copies already shared with others are separate.

Profile name and photo

Apple may provide your name when you first authorize the app. Sign in with Apple does not expose your Apple Account profile photo. You can choose a photo in NutriGo and sync it with your account.

Nutrition and illustrations

Nutrition figures are estimates when identified as such. Check the displayed source and ingredient labels. Catalog photos are AI-generated illustrations and may differ from the result you prepare. NutriGo does not provide medical advice.
